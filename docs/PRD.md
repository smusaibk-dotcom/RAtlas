## Project Overview

Ratlas is an AI-Powered Application that Transforms fragmented research into an interactive Evolution Graph , helping Researchers understand how ideas evolved, what problems they solved and what Challenges remain.This Application also helps Researchers Summarize or Explain a research paper in the form of docs, an image or a video in their preferred way of learning connecting learning with a Fun Experience.

## Problem Statement

A Researcher often Struggles to understand how a particular field evolved because the knowledge around the field is often Scattered in books, Research Papers, Blogs, Internet, Videos, Code Repositories etc. Research is Mostly Non Linear

Existing AI tools Answer Isolated Questions and unless Explicitly Stated to do so, they Struggle to connect the dots between a concept and how it evolved over the time, making it difficult for aa pperson to identify Evolution Pattern, the Problems solved over time,  breakthroughs and remainig research gaps.

## Core User Journey
Primary Journey (Core Product)
- User Inputs a Research Topic
- Ratlas Collects trusted Research Sources
- Extracts the Knowledge from the Sources
- Builds an Interactive Research Evolution in the Form of an Evolution Tree
- User Explores the Evolution tree or nodes of their interest
Secondary Journey
- User Inputs a document/Image/Video along with their Query
- Ratlas Retrives the information from the Upload
- Extracts the Knowledge from the Other Sources if required
- Answers the User Query in the form of text or An interactive Graph

## Core Features
- Research Topic Explorer
- Research Evolution Generator
- Research Paper Simplifier
- Multimodal Knowledge Extraction
